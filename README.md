## About

I'm **Mirza Şimşek** — a **Management Information Systems** student and product engineer at **[CodionX](https://codionx.com)**. I work where AI, product design and full-stack systems meet.

I've been making software and digital products since childhood. The work has moved through web and mobile products, marketplaces, research agents, image intelligence, original block libraries and interaction systems—but the impulse has stayed the same: take the idea seriously enough to make it real.

> **I don't collect demos. I build the system behind the demo.**

## Selected work

- **[Buildary](https://buildary.dev)** — Türkiye's first open-source, AI-native web builder.
- **[NeedGo](https://github.com/aethrixn/needgo)** — a reverse marketplace built in public.
- **Spatial AI** — room redesign and arrangement from a single photograph.
- **Visual commerce** — better environments for vehicle listings and studio photography.
- **Research agents** — evidence-first systems for articles, research and thesis workflows.
- **Interface systems** — original block libraries, design systems and interaction experiments.

I also explored market tools, faith and daily-practice apps, social products and dozens of interface concepts along the way. Before “Study Mode” became a familiar AI product pattern, I was already arguing in Türkiye for AI experiences that teach, question and scaffold thinking instead of only returning an answer.
